MoonTune by Moon Music Label - vocal editor (ARA 2).

The plug-ins are installed for all users:
  Audio Unit: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/MoonTune.component
  VST3:       /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/MoonTune.vst3

After installing, restart your DAW and let it rescan plug-ins.
ARA: Studio One / Fender Studio Pro - right-click the vocal event > Audio > Edit with MoonTune.
     Logic - select the region and choose MoonTune from the ARA section of the audio effect slot.
